## Notes

- The four Vitaly Gariev images are staged stock photographs (they appear to be stills from stock video shoots). Unsplash shows no camera data for them. They were checked at full size for signs of generated imagery and none were found, but they are the first candidates to replace with real photography.
- The skylines (Dallas, New York) are used as context for services, not as office locations. Do not caption them as offices.
- Real photography of the team, offices and member firms will always look more credible than stock images. Replace these as soon as it is available, and add the new files here.

## Fonts

Newsreader and Hanken Grotesk are used under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The license texts are in `src/assets/fonts/`. Hanken Grotesk is self-hosted through `next/font/google`. Newsreader is self-hosted from `src/assets/fonts/Newsreader-Display-Regular.woff2`, the display optical size at regular weight, converted from the TTF in the same folder (Newsreader declares no Reserved Font Name, so the converted file keeps its name). The TTF files are also used to render the share image and were used to outline the wordmark.
